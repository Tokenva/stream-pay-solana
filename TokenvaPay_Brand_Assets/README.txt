TOKENVAPAY BRAND ASSETS

This product logo is designed to complement the supplied Tokenva brand assets. It reuses the rounded-square and three-bar geometry from the company's supplied symbol, with a mint-green product accent.

FILES
- tokenvapay-logo-dark.svg / .png: white wordmark with mint-green “Pay”, for dark backgrounds
- tokenvapay-logo-light.svg / .png: black wordmark with green “Pay”, for light backgrounds
- tokenvapay-symbol-*.svg: standalone vector symbol variants
- tokenvapay-app-icon-dark/light.svg and PNG: app icon variants
- tokenvapay-favicon-*.png: favicon/app sizes
- tokenvapay-symbol-*.png: transparent standalone mark raster exports

SVG files are scalable source assets. Wordmark text remains live text in SVG; for guaranteed identical rendering across systems, convert text to outlines in a vector editor before commercial print use.

Brand guidance: keep the company name Tokenva distinct from the product name TokenvaPay. Do not use the product logo as a replacement for the corporate Tokenva logo.
